Cohort v1

Refund policy

The production product is configured, but the cohort has not opened to buyers yet. Once checkout is activated, refund handling will use the selected Merchant of Record's supported process and applicable consumer-law requirements.

Principle

Refund and chargeback requests are handled through the payment provider's supported process. We do not rely on an absolute no-refund rule.

Defective delivery

If a buyer cannot access the purchased product or the shipped product has a reproducible material defect, support will first attempt a prompt delivery or defect remedy. Provider-required refund rights remain available.

Recordkeeping

Only the minimum transaction/support metadata needed to resolve and audit the issue is retained by the seller.
