Not a replacement for Seller Center. Not another giant spreadsheet.

The War Room sits between native platform reporting and a full recurring SaaS stack: a small operator decision layer focused on what is safe to do next.

Seller Center / GMV Max

Strong for platform-level traffic, GMV, ad optimization and affiliate reporting. The War Room adds the business-level check: COGS/variable cost, creator/sample cost, returns/refunds and stock risk before SCALE / HOLD / FIX / STOP.

Spreadsheet

Flexible and free, but easily becomes a manual archive. The War Room constrains the inputs, calculates contribution consistently, applies explicit decision gates and preserves why the operator changed course.

Profit / affiliate SaaS

Dedicated SaaS is better once automatic multi-source data and enterprise workflow justify recurring cost. The War Room targets the operator who is too complex for one sheet but not yet ready for several $30–$200+/month tools.

Stop condition

If realistic daily refresh takes more than 15 minutes, or users cannot make a different decision after seeing the cockpit, this product should be downgraded rather than defended.
