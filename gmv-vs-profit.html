Sales can go up while your business gets worse.

GMV is a top-line measure. Before scaling, check whether contribution remains positive after paid spend, refunds and variable cost — and whether stock and return risk can support more volume.

Before SCALE

  • Observed contribution is positive.
  • Evidence quality meets the minimum window.
  • Inventory is above the safety floor.
  • Return rate is within the configured tolerance.
  • Execution capacity exists.

If any gate fails

HOLD when evidence is weak. FIX when a reversible bottleneck exists. STOP when contribution remains negative and no fixable blocker remains.
