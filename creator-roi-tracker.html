Which creators are actually profitable?

Creator GMV is not the same as creator contribution after samples, commission, returns and product economics.

What the War Room keeps together

Creator/content asset → campaign → SKU → attribution strength → direct creator cost → observed contribution → stock/return risk → next operator action.

Do not force attribution

If attribution is missing, the product leaves creator ROI blank or weak instead of inventing certainty from views, likes or correlation.

Decision outcome

The useful question is not “which creator has the most GMV?” but “which creator/SKU combination should I test, hold, fix or stop next?”
