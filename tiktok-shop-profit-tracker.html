Track the numbers that decide whether a campaign is safe to scale: gross sales, paid spend, refunds/cancellations, variable cost, stock coverage and return risk.

The core formula

contribution = gross sales - refunds/cancellations - paid spend - variable cost

Then apply business risk

Positive contribution alone is not enough. SCALE only when stock is safe, return risk is acceptable and evidence quality meets your configured minimum. Otherwise HOLD, FIX or STOP.
