Cohort v1

Privacy notice

This static prelaunch site has no advertising pixels or account login. A privacy-oriented cohort measurement module is present but remains disabled while its public analytics endpoint is empty; in that state it sends no analytics request and writes no QV timestamp.

Cohort measurement when activated

The first cohort is designed to count only qualified visits from predeclared acquisition sources. A first-party browser timestamp suppresses repeat counting for 24 hours; it does not contain an email, order/customer ID or financial value. Qualified events are planned to be sent to a cookie-free GoatCounter endpoint. Internal/test traffic and obvious automation are excluded. This measurement is not used for advertising profiles or cross-site tracking.

Template data

The demo template contains fictional data. Buyers replace it with their own information inside their own Notion workspace or local Web core.

Seller access

The base product is designed so the seller does not need access to a buyer's Notion workspace, ad accounts, payment accounts or customer lists.

Support data

Support requests should contain only the minimum information needed to reproduce a product issue and should prefer fictional or redacted examples. Do not send passwords, cookies, bank details, payment credentials or unnecessary personal data.

Checkout data

Once checkout is activated, payment and transaction data are handled by the selected Merchant of Record under its privacy terms. Only minimum transaction/support metadata needed for fulfillment, refunds, disputes and cohort measurement is retained by the seller.
