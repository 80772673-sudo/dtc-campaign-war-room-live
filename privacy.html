Privacy notice

This static prelaunch site does not contain analytics, advertising pixels, account login or a checkout script. Checkout data will be handled by the selected Merchant of Record once activated.

Template data

The demo template contains fictional data. Buyers replace it with their own information inside their own Notion workspace or local Web core.

Seller access

The base product is designed so the seller does not need access to a buyer's Notion workspace, ad accounts, payment accounts or customer lists.

Support data

Support requests should contain only the minimum information needed to reproduce a product issue and should prefer fictional or redacted examples. Do not send passwords, cookies, bank details, payment credentials or unnecessary personal data.

Checkout data

Once checkout is activated, payment and transaction data are handled by the selected Merchant of Record under its privacy terms. Only minimum transaction/support metadata needed for fulfillment, refunds, disputes and cohort measurement is retained by the seller.
